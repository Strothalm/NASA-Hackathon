Formulas used

Total mass = all part masses + fuel. Thrust-to-weight ratio = thrust / (total mass × g0), g0 = 9.80665 m/s².
Delta-v = Isp × g0 × ln(start mass / end mass) (ideal rocket equation), see NASA Glenn Research Center: Ideal Rocket Equation.
Effective data = data × (1 − loss in %).
