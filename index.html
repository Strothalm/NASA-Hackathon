Mission Design Game: Tools

Two small web tools for our board and card game (NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, challenge "Space Mission Design Game").

  • Mission Calculator: type in the values from your cards and check mass, cost, power, thrust and data.
  • Asteroid Risk Meter: watch the danger level while you drill. Leave in time.

Code generated with AI assistance (Claude by Anthropic). Game idea, rules and values by the team. Numbers are placeholders until playtesting.
